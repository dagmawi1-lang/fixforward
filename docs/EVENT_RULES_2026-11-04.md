# STEMergent Hacks 2026 rules checked November 4, 2026

- Competition dates: October 30 through November 15, 2026.
- Current Devpost deadline: November 15, 2026 at 9:30 PM Central (check the listing again before submitting).
- Eligibility: currently enrolled middle-school, high-school, or college students who meet organizer age requirements. Solo or teams up to four; each participant may be on one team and each team/participant may submit one project.
- Pathways: Ideathon (idea/concept) or Hackathon (working prototype/application/device).
- Projects must address a meaningful problem for students, schools, nonprofits, communities, or another group with a clear need.
- AI tools, open-source libraries, APIs, frameworks, datasets, and public resources are allowed. Participants must understand their work and disclose significant AI/third-party contributions when requested.
- Existing projects may be a starting point, but the submission must include meaningful new work completed for the competition.
