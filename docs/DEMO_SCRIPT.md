# FixForward demo script — 2 minutes (v1.2.0)

1. **Problem (15s):** Small public-space problems get noticed by many people but reported in scattered, duplicate, or vague ways, and nobody can see what happens next.
2. **Board (15s):** Show the fictional board, the resolution progress bar, and the category and status filters.
3. **Duplicate check (25s):** Start reporting "Broken light on walking path" at "East walking loop". FixForward finds the existing "Pathway light is not working" report. Click **Support this instead** and point out that its priority went up and no duplicate was created.
4. **New report (15s):** Add a genuinely new observation, such as cracked bench slats in the south picnic area, using a general public area and no personal details.
5. **Explainable priority (20s):** Open **Details** and read the "Why priority …?" breakdown: severity, accessibility, and capped community support.
6. **Follow-through (15s):** Change the demo status to Assigned and show the status history timeline.
7. **Hand-off (10s):** Click **Action brief** to download a plain-text, priority-ordered list a facilities or parks team can act on. CSV and JSON exports are also available.
8. **Limits (5s):** No municipal integration yet, browser-local votes (not tamper-proof), and not for emergencies.
